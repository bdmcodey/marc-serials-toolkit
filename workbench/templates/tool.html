Every group of holdings that shares a structure gets one expression. Patterns whose captured values could all be read are confirmed for you; the rest ask what a value means. Confirmed patterns are used when you convert, and anything they do not match is converted the way it always was.

Every pattern confirmed so far, kept for this visit. They are tried in turn before the standard parser, so what is listed here is what will read your holdings. Remove any that should not be used, or export them to load again next time.
