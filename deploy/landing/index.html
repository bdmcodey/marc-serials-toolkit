MARC Serials Toolkit

Tools for turning free-text serials holdings into structured MARC fields.

Proof of concept, under active development. Check the output before loading it into the catalog.

Holdings Workbench start here /workbench

Finds the recurring patterns in a file of 866 holdings, asks you to confirm what each part of a pattern means, and converts them to 853 / 863 — showing the fields each record will get before anything is written. Holdings no pattern matches are converted the way the Converter has always converted them.

Converter /converter

Turns a textual 866 holdings statement — or a whole MARC file — into structured 853 caption/pattern and 863 enumeration/chronology fields.

Pattern Detector /patterns

Groups a collection of 866 statements by their structure and generates a named-group regular expression for each pattern it finds.

The Converter and the Pattern Detector each do one half of the job and are still the right tool when that half is all you need. The Workbench is the two joined up: the patterns it finds are what does the converting.
